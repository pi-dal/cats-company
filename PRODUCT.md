# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who delegate real work to AI employees from the WebApp or chat while moving between phones, personal computers, workstations, and cloud hosts. They need to understand which authorized device will perform a task and whether an important result reached them when the app was not in the foreground.

## Product Purpose

CatsCo is the identity, messaging, collaboration, and device control plane for AI employees. It lets users assign goals, route supported work to explicitly selected and currently authorized devices, and receive results in the originating conversation without treating the product as a remote desktop.

## Brand Personality

Capable, direct, and trustworthy. The interface should feel like a focused work tool: clear about system state and authorization boundaries, calm during long-running work, and precise when delivery or device availability is uncertain.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a remote-desktop console, a consumer social feed, or a decorative AI demo. Avoid implying that every device is interchangeable, that background delivery is guaranteed, or that shared access to an AI employee grants access to another person's devices.

## Design Principles

1. Put the active task and conversation ahead of product explanation.
2. Make identity, device, and authorization boundaries visible at the point of action.
3. Report observable system state without overstating reliability or support.
4. Keep frequent workflows compact, familiar, and usable from mobile devices.
5. Treat trust, privacy, and recoverability as product behavior rather than legal footnotes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, descriptive accessible names, and status feedback that does not rely on color alone. Respect reduced-motion preferences, maintain readable contrast across supported themes, and keep controls usable on narrow touch screens. Use plain Chinese for the primary interface while preserving the existing internationalization structure where available.
